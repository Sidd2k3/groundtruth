# Lumen Freight Co. — Leave Policy

Policy owner: People Operations. Effective date: 1 March 2025. This is a fictional policy used as a demo corpus.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees accrue 22 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 1.83 days per month of service. Part-time employees accrue annual leave pro rata based on their contracted hours.

After five years of continuous service, the annual entitlement increases to 25 days. After ten years of continuous service, the annual entitlement increases to 28 days.

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March of the following year, after which they expire. Unused leave beyond the carry-over limit is forfeited and is not paid out, except on termination of employment.

Annual leave requests of more than five consecutive working days must be submitted at least four weeks in advance. Requests of five days or fewer must be submitted at least three working days in advance. Managers must respond to leave requests within five working days.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Unused sick leave does not carry over to the following year.

A medical certificate is required for any absence of more than three consecutive days. Employees must notify their manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.

## Parental Leave

The primary caregiver is entitled to 26 weeks of parental leave, of which the first 16 weeks are paid at full salary and the remaining 10 weeks are unpaid. The secondary caregiver is entitled to 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

To be eligible for paid parental leave, an employee must have completed at least 6 months of continuous service before the expected date of birth or adoption.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees are entitled to 5 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member, and 2 days on the death of an extended family member. Immediate family includes a spouse or partner, child, parent, or sibling.

## Unpaid Leave and Sabbaticals

Employees with at least three years of continuous service may apply for an unpaid sabbatical of up to 3 months. Sabbatical requests must be approved by both the employee's manager and the Head of People Operations. Only one sabbatical may be taken in any five-year period.
