# Lumen Freight Co. — Information Security Policy

Policy owner: IT Security. Effective date: 15 February 2025. This is a fictional policy used as a demo corpus.

## Passwords and Authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords must be changed only when there is evidence of compromise; routine periodic password changes are no longer required. Passwords must not be reused across company and personal accounts.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, VPN, and all cloud applications. Hardware security keys are required for administrators and members of the Finance team. SMS codes are not an approved second factor.

## Devices

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption. Screens must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Security updates must be installed within 7 days of release, and critical security updates within 48 hours.

Personal devices may access company email only through the approved mobile management app. Personal devices must not be used to store customer data.

## Data Classification

Company data is classified into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted. Customer shipment records are classified as Confidential. Payroll and health data are classified as Restricted. Restricted data must not be stored on removable media such as USB drives.

## Incident Reporting

Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team within 1 hour of discovery by emailing security@lumenfreight.example or calling the internal hotline on extension 4400. Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately. Employees must not attempt to investigate an incident themselves.

## Access Reviews

Managers must review their team's access rights every quarter. Access for departing employees must be revoked by the end of their last working day.

## Security Training

All employees must complete security awareness training within 30 days of joining and then annually. Simulated phishing exercises are run every month. Employees who fail two simulated phishing exercises in a row must complete a refresher course.
