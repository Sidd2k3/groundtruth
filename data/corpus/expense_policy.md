# Lumen Freight Co. — Travel and Expense Policy

Policy owner: Finance. Effective date: 1 January 2025. This is a fictional policy used as a demo corpus.

## Submitting Expenses

All expense claims must be submitted through the Ledgerly expense system within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 90 days will not be reimbursed. An itemised receipt is required for every expense over 25 euros.

Approved claims are reimbursed in the next monthly payroll run. Claims approved after the 20th of the month are paid in the following month's payroll.

## Approval Limits

Expenses up to 500 euros may be approved by the employee's direct manager. Expenses between 500 and 5,000 euros require approval from the department head. Expenses over 5,000 euros require approval from the Chief Financial Officer.

## Air Travel

Economy class must be used for all flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is permitted for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires prior written approval from the Chief Financial Officer. Flights should be booked at least 14 days before departure whenever possible.

## Accommodation

The nightly hotel limit is 180 euros in standard cities and 260 euros in high-cost cities. High-cost cities are London, Paris, Zurich, New York, and Tokyo. Hotel bookings exceeding the limit require department head approval before booking.

## Meals and Per Diem

When travelling overnight for business, employees receive a meal per diem of 60 euros per day. On the first and last day of a trip, the per diem is reduced to 45 euros. Alcohol is not reimbursable, except when entertaining clients with prior approval from the department head.

## Ground Transport and Mileage

Employees using a personal car for business travel are reimbursed at 0.30 euros per kilometre. Taxis and ride-hailing services are reimbursable when public transport is not practical or after 22:00.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

The following are not reimbursable: traffic and parking fines, personal entertainment, airline seat upgrades, gym or spa charges, and lost luggage that was not reported to the airline.
