# Lumen Freight Co. — Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Policy owner: People Operations. Effective date: 1 April 2025. This is a fictional policy used as a demo corpus.

## Eligibility

Employees become eligible for hybrid work after completing their 3-month probation period. Warehouse and fleet operations roles are not eligible for remote work because they require on-site presence.

## Hybrid Schedule

Hybrid employees must work from the office at least 2 days per week. Each team sets one shared anchor day per week on which all team members attend the office. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time, during which employees must be reachable.

## Working Abroad

Employees may work remotely from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Working abroad requires approval from the employee's manager and from the Legal team at least 2 weeks before travel. Working abroad is not permitted from countries subject to international sanctions.

## Home Office Equipment

Eligible employees receive a one-time home office allowance of 400 euros for furniture and equipment. In addition, hybrid employees receive an internet allowance of 25 euros per month, paid through payroll. Equipment bought with the allowance remains the employee's property.

## Health and Safety

Employees working from home must complete the home workstation self-assessment within the first month of starting hybrid work. Work-related injuries at home must be reported to People Operations within 24 hours.

## Changes to Arrangements

The company may require an employee to return to full-time office work with 4 weeks' notice if business needs change or if performance concerns arise.
